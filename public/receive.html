Capturing data from bioluminescent waves

How to use:
1. Click "Start Camera" and allow camera access
2. Select Binary or Quaternary mode (match transmitter)
3. Click "Start Capturing" to begin listening
4. Point camera at transmitting screen
5. Look for:
  • Long GREEN pulse = transmission starting
  • Flashing colors = data being sent
  • Long PURPLE pulse = transmission complete
6. Message will auto-decode when complete!
