How it works:

2. Click "Start Receiving" to capture emoji particles
3. Camera auto-detects color+emoji (redundant encoding!)
4. Manually enter direction you see (↑↓←→)
5. Direction (2 bits) + Color/Emoji (2 bits) = 4 bits per particle
6. Message auto-decodes as particles are captured!
